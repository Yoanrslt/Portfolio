Education Sept. 2025 – 2027

MSc in Tissue and Implant Bioengineering

Aix-Marseille University · Faculty of Sport Sciences

A multidisciplinary programme, between industry and research, to design and evaluate implantable medical devices.

The programme

The Tissue and Implant Bioengineering track of the IEAP Master's trains engineers to develop implants and medical devices that restore biomechanical function. It combines knowledge of the human body (functional anatomy, biomechanics, neuroscience) with applied engineering: motion capture, numerical modelling and biomaterials.

It is backed by the ISM laboratory (Institute of Movement Sciences, AMU & CNRS) and the IML (Marseille Institute of the Locomotor System, AP-HM), with researchers, clinicians and industry professionals teaching.

What I learn

  • Analyse: biomechanics, neurophysiology, behavioural neuroscience, musculoskeletal disorders, mechanobiology, biomaterial physics and biocompatibility, biotribology, fluid mechanics and haemodynamics.
  • Design: CAD and numerical modelling (SolidWorks, Abaqus, 3-matic), finite element analysis, 3D segmentation, instrumentation and measurement, signal processing, programming (Python, MATLAB), statistics, artificial intelligence, medical imaging.
  • Deliver: clinical evaluation design for implantable devices, implant manufacturing and industrial deployment, regulatory affairs (MDR 2017/745, ISO 13485, ISO 17025), project and team management, project work in English.
