Experience Apr. – Aug. 2026

Quality Engineering Intern

HTI Technologies · HTI Group

Building the quality management system of AURA Lab, the group's future calibration and testing laboratory, towards COFRAC accreditation under ISO/IEC 17025, and digitalising quality processes.

Context

HTI Group (HTI Technologies, HTI Med and AURA Lab) designs and manufactures plasma-sprayed coatings, bioceramics and components for implantable medical devices. In this sector, document control and traceability are essential to comply with ISO 13485, ISO/IEC 17025 and the MDR 2017/745.

My internship was part of the creation of AURA Lab, a future calibration and testing laboratory whose quality system had to be built from scratch on the Qualnet platform, towards COFRAC accreditation.

Missions

  • ISO/IEC 17025 document system: folder structure, user groups, access-rights matrix, document coding, approval cycle; writing, revising and importing quality documents.
  • Process digitalisation: designing and deploying Qualnet workflows (moderator tickets, workstation audits, anomalies) with the departments involved, to standardise processes, cut redundant manual entry and improve traceability.
  • Redesign of the Qualnet portal and of the document management system's usability, together with process owners.
  • Workstation audits in production: reports, corrective and preventive action plans (CAPA), follow-up with process owners.
  • Promoting a quality culture: presenting findings in cross-department meetings and following up actions to control non-conformity risk.

Method and results

I organised the work around the PDCA cycle, the reference method in quality engineering cited by ISO 13485, with tracking indicators: document compliance rate (TCD), audit completion rate (TRA), CAPA closure rate and average processing time.

After several beta-testing phases, the moderator-ticket and workstation-audit workflows were fully operational by the end of the internship.

Key takeaways

Hands-on experience of a demanding, regulated biomedical manufacturing environment: balancing standards, the teams' operational needs and competitiveness, and getting everyone on board with new tools.
