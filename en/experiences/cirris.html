Experience Jan. – May 2025

Research Intern in Neuromechanics

CIRRIS · Interdisciplinary Research Centre in Rehabilitation and Social Integration

MATLAB processing and analysis of neuromechanical data (cutaneous reflex, intermuscular coherence, sensorimotor mechanisms), in collaboration with Université Polytechnique Hauts-de-France (Valenciennes).

Context

During my exchange year at Université Laval, I joined Prof. Martin Simoneau's research team; I was also taking his courses on kinetic and kinematic analysis and on neuromechanical signal processing. The project studied sensorimotor mechanisms: cutaneous reflex, the role of mechanoreceptors and intermuscular coherence.

Missions

  • Literature review to identify the most relevant experimental protocols and signal processing methods.
  • Adapting and using MATLAB scripts to process and analyse EMG signals, and contributing to writing them.
  • Organising and structuring large datasets for reproducible analyses.
  • Regular discussions with researchers on methodological choices and the interpretation of preliminary results.

Key takeaways

Research in an international, inter-university setting, a demand for reproducible analyses and the habit of keeping up with the scientific literature.
