Experience Apr. – May 2024

Research Intern in Biomechanics

LIBM · Inter-university Laboratory of Human Movement Biology

Independently designed a protocol and a measurement setup to assess the validity and reliability of a force-velocity test on a vertical pull movement.

Université Savoie Mont Blanc campus in Le Bourget-du-Lac, home of the LIBM. Photo: Florian Pépellin, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (cropped)

Context

An internship I sought out myself, on a project led by Prof. Pierre Samozino: developing a new method to assess force-velocity relationships in an upper-limb pulling movement.

Missions

  • Literature review on force-velocity profiles to define the key protocol parameters.
  • Designing a protocol and measurement setup to assess the validity and reliability of a vertical pull test, with standardised measurement conditions.
  • Finding, screening and recruiting about thirty participants, in line with ethics and representativeness.
  • Handling sensors and measurements, and processing the data.

Key takeaways

Running a study on my own, from research question to data: a first complete experience of experimental methodology and instrumentation.
