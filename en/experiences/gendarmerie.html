Experience Dec. 2019 – Jul. 2023

Gendarmerie Non-Commissioned Officer

French National Gendarmerie · Mobile Gendarmerie

Patrols and public order operations in sensitive areas, with long deployments in French Guiana and New Caledonia: four years of acting under pressure, team leadership and strict procedures.

Missions

  • Patrols and public order in sensitive areas, with long deployments of up to 4 months overseas (French Guiana, New Caledonia).
  • Leadership of up to 30 people in a high-stakes security environment; supervising and planning training to build team cohesion.
  • Autonomous decisions in critical, high-pressure situations to keep operations running and personnel safe.
  • Traceability lead: reliable reports and strict adherence to internal procedures.
  • Escorting high-risk detainees to court, supervising cadets at a Gendarmerie school, presenting the Mobile Gendarmerie to young people.

Why a career change?

I loved the field work, the team and the sense of service, but I missed intellectual challenge. My passion for sport and my appetite for science led me to bioengineering, with the same drive: to be useful, close to people.

Key takeaways

Calm and method when safety is at stake, clear communication with very different people (chain of command, civilian partners, teams) and a culture of procedure and traceability that I find again today in quality and regulatory work.
